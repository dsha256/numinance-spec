# Governance

- Everything in this directory is licensed under Apache-2.0, including its patent grant. Numinance commits not to relicense released versions of the protocol, SDKs, conformance kits, CLI or official adapters under a more restrictive license.
- Changes beyond `contract-additive` go through an RFC in `rfcs/` (template `rfcs/0000-template.md`), open for comment for at least 14 days before acceptance.
- Contributions use the Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO, `Signed-off-by`). No CLA.
- "Numinance", "Numinance Verified" and "Numinance Conformant" are trademarks; use is governed by `TRADEMARKS.md` (to be published with the registry).
- Decision-making is by the maintainers listed in `MAINTAINERS.md` until a neutral foundation is considered (planned once three or more external vendors ship adapters).
